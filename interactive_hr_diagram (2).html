How the properties are estimated The widget uses simple astrophysical approximations suitable for teaching and outreach.

Radius: computed from the Stefan–Boltzmann relation in solar units, \(L/L_\odot = (R/R_\odot)^2 (T/T_\odot)^4\).

Colour: estimated from temperature using a smooth blackbody-inspired mapping.

Constant-radius lines: plotted from \(L/L_\odot = (R/R_\odot)^2 (T/T_\odot)^4\), so on a log-luminosity scale they form sloping lines.

Nearest example: found by comparing your chosen point to the stored example stars in log-temperature and log-luminosity space.

Important: not every clicked point corresponds to a physically common or stable star, but it is useful for exploring the diagram.
