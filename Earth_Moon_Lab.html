01Begin at new Moon

The Moon lies in the Sunward direction. The dashed star line and golden Sun line initially coincide.

02Stop after 27.32 days

The Moon completes 360°. But Earth has moved around the Sun, turning the Sunward line by about 27°. The Moon is still a waning crescent.

03Continue to 29.53 days

The Moon catches up with the moving Sunward line. Another new Moon arrives, about 2.21 days after the sidereal month ends.

About the model & teaching notes

These are circular, coplanar, constant-speed teaching models viewed from the north. Sizes and distances are not to scale. The rotation demonstration isolates spin and orbital motion; its hemisphere colours identify the surface, not illumination. It omits libration, eccentricity, axial tilt and Earth’s daily spin. The months demonstration uses a mean sidereal period of 27.32166 days and a year of 365.25636 days, giving a synodic period of approximately 29.53059 days. Its Sun rays are treated as parallel across the lunar orbit. The Sun icon in the overview is not at the scale of the main diagram. The phase disc is schematic; new-Moon alignments do not imply an eclipse in the real, tilted lunar orbit. The real near side is not always sunlit, and the far side is not permanently dark.
