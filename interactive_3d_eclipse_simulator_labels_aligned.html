Using the eclipse simulator

The faint blue plane is the ecliptic. The purple plane is the Moon’s tilted orbital plane. Their intersection is the line of nodes.

  • A solar eclipse requires the Moon to be near new Moon and near a node.
  • A lunar eclipse requires the Moon to be near full Moon and near a node.
  • The dark central cone is the umbra. The wider translucent volume is the penumbra. Beyond the tip of the Moon’s umbra is the antumbra, where an annular eclipse is possible.
  • Teaching scale exaggerates sizes and compresses distances. Proportional bodies gives a more realistic Earth–Moon size and distance ratio while keeping the Sun close enough to navigate.
